The Setup

The Inflatable Backdrop

This isn’t a curtain on a flimsy frame. It’s a full inflatable backdrop booth — a soft, enclosed shell that your guests step into. It inflates into shape and holds itself up, which is why it works in places a traditional booth can’t go.

What that means for your photos: the background is clean and identical in every single shot. No cluttered wall behind it, no ceiling lights blowing out half the frame, no shadows from somebody’s phone. The booth lights the shot the same way at 6 PM and at midnight, so the photos from the first hour and the last hour actually look like they belong together.

It also sets up almost anywhere — tight banquet rooms, barns, tents, a driveway, out on the lawn — because it doesn’t need a wall to hang anything on and there’s no rig to trip over. And because the walls are inflated, it’s forgiving in a crowded room.
