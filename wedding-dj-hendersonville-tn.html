Meet Your DJ

More Than a DJ — Your Night's Host

AS·U·LIKE·IT Entertainment is a Hendersonville, Tennessee based wedding and event DJ company with 15+ years of experience and 900+ events under our belt. We bring the party — professional MC services, custom playlists built around your taste, state-of-the-art sound and lighting, and an optional photo booth that keeps guests smiling all night.

What sets us apart: we don't just play songs. We read the room, keep the timeline moving, work seamlessly with your venue and planner, and make sure the dance floor is full from the first song to the last.

We serve Hendersonville, Gallatin, Goodlettsville, Sumner County, and all of Middle Tennessee — and we're happy to travel.
