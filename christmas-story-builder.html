Host instructions

Read this part to the room before you begin.

  1. When you hear LEFT, pass your gift one person to your left. When you hear RIGHT, pass it one person to your right. Wait for each cue, and pass gently!
  2. Give one practice cue in each direction before starting.
  3. The story title and these instructions are not part of the game. Only begin passing when you announce that the story has started.
  4. Read slowly and pause after each bold direction. Don’t count words such as “alright” or “leftovers” as cues.
  5. After the final sentence, announce: “Stop passing!”
  6. Guests holding the designated gifts keep them. Confirm the number of gifts and any additional prizes with the organizer beforehand.
  7. For a larger group, use several smaller circles so passing stays manageable. Seated participation is always an option.
Practice cue: “Everybody pass LEFT… now pass RIGHT.” Then start the story.
