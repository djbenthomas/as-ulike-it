Questions

Before You Book

What’s the difference between the $950 and the $600 package?

Who’s behind the booth. The $950 is Benny personally — he built AS·U·LIKE·IT and has played 900+ events. The $600 is one of our team DJs running the same show standard with the same gear and the same planning. Both are up to six hours.

What if my reception runs longer than six hours?

Ask — we quote extra time by the hour rather than building it into a padded package price.

Is the photo booth $200 on its own?

No. $200 is the add-on price when it’s booked with a DJ package. A booth-only booking is priced separately.

Do you travel outside Hendersonville?

Yes — all of Middle Tennessee, including Gallatin, Goodlettsville, Nashville, Mt. Juliet, Lebanon, Springfield and Portland. We’ve traveled much further than that, including California and back.

How far out should we book?

Popular Saturdays go six to twelve months out, especially May, June, September and October. If your date is close, still ask — cancellations happen.

How do we hold our date?

Send the inquiry with your date and venue, and we’ll confirm availability and the deposit terms in writing before anything is due.
