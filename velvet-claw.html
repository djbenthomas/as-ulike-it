Hey — I'm Bailey

I'm Bailey Cody, a Nashville DJ performing as Velvet Claw. I put together sets for Broadway restaurants, hotel lounges, and private events — from laid-back dinner music to upbeat late-night favorites.

That's the whole job, honestly. A room at 7pm and the same room at 11pm are two different rooms, and the set has to move with them. I build it from my own taste, not a shuffled playlist, and I stay in it the whole night: dancing behind the booth, smiling, and taking requests from whoever walks up.

What the night sounds like

Dinner serviceLow, warm, and easy to talk over. Nobody has to raise their voice to order.
The turnWhen the plates stop coming — familiar songs people sing without thinking.
Late nightUpbeat favorites, requests off the crowd, and the songs that close a room down right.
