How the Christmas Mystery Works

A Whodunit Your Team Actually Plays

We bring a hosted Christmas murder mystery dinner-party experience to your location. Guests work together to gather clues, question suspicious characters, and solve a funny holiday whodunit — while enjoying their company dinner.

1

Arrive & Settle In

Your team arrives, grabs dinner, and gets pulled into the story within minutes. No awkward ice-breaking — the mystery does it for you.

2

Meet the Suspects

Suspicious characters work the room. Guests question them, compare notes, and start building a theory of the case.

3

Gather Clues

Team challenges and short games feed new evidence to the room. Nobody sits on the sidelines watching.

4

Name the Culprit

Everyone locks in their accusation, the truth comes out, and the winning table takes the bragging rights back to the office.

Christmas Murder Mystery is our main product — an original script we’re developing right now for this season. Themes we’re working on include “Who Killed Santa?” and “Who Killed the Reindeer?”

If you’re planning early, tell us the date and we’ll confirm exactly which story is running for your party.
